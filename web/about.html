About

Hi. I'm the beaver.

I chew PDFs, Word files and slides into Markdown an AI can actually digest. I keep the headings, tables and formulas, and spit out the page numbers, running headers and other wood chips — so the same document costs fewer tokens.

It's free. I work for sticks.

How I work

  1. 1
    Your browser takes the first bite. Text PDFs, Word and PowerPoint are converted on your device and never leave it.
  2. 2
    The tough bits go to my GPU. Scanned pages, two-column layouts and big tables are read on a GPU. Pick Best quality and every page goes there.
  3. 3
    Nothing gets stashed in the dam. Uploads are deleted right after conversion. Pick Device only and nothing is uploaded at all.

Fewer tokens, nearly the same answers

−46%tokens compared with handing the AI your pages as images, at 94% vs 98% correct answers.

83 questions about 11 documents (31 pages of Korean, Chinese and English: text, scans, handwriting, slides), answered by a local 27B model that saw only one version of each document. Both mdbeaver rows keep page numbers. Most of the gap to page images comes from questions about what is drawn inside figures. It's a small test; the scripts are on GitHub.

What happens to your files

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics.
  • Uploaded files are deleted as soon as they are converted; results are gone within 10 minutes.
  • Pages that need the GPU are sent over HTTPS and deleted once they are read.
  • The web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, page) for 14 days.

A full privacy policy and terms of use are on the way.
